Questions

Is Flow Net Worth free?

Yes. Flow Net Worth is free to use, with no card required and no ads.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can start immediately and your data stays in your browser. If you create an account later, everything you entered moves into it and syncs across your devices.

Does Flow Net Worth connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank login or bank-connection service. You add transactions yourself in a few seconds, or import them from a CSV file exported by your bank.

Is it safe to add my crypto wallet?

Flow Net Worth only reads balances from a public Bitcoin or Solana address. It never asks for private keys or seed phrases and cannot move funds.

Can it track pay from hourly or cash work?

Yes. Log the hours you work, and on payday enter what you were actually paid. The difference from the estimate is booked automatically.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Settings has a one-click CSV export and a permanent delete. Your data is never sold, and each account's rows are isolated with row-level security.
